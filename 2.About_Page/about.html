About Zaraf Multiverse

Discover our journey, our vision for the future, and the leadership driving our multi-industry success.

Who We Are

An Integrated Ecosystem

Zaraf Multiverse Group is a modern, diversified, and forward-looking business group committed to creating a positive impact on individuals, organizations, and the overall economy by delivering integrated services across multiple sectors.

Established in Bangladesh, the group is gradually working toward building a strong multinational business network.

The core objective of the group is to develop an integrated ecosystem across sectors such as technology, infrastructure, transportation, real estate, and business consultancy. Each sister concern operates with its own expertise, but all share a common goal ensuring quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Journey

Our journey officially began on January 25, 2024, with a vision to build a multi-sector business group. The company formally started its operations through a written agreement on January 19, 2025.

Since then, we have launched multiple ventures including transport, consultancy, and technology services, and continue to grow steadily.

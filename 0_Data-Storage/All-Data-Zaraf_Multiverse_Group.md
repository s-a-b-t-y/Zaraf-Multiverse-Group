# Zaraf Multiverse Group – All Information Area

## About Company

Zaraf Multiverse Group is a modern, diversified, and forward-looking business group committed to creating a positive impact on individuals, organizations, and the overall economy by delivering integrated services across multiple sectors. Established in Bangladesh, the group is gradually working toward building a strong multinational business network.

The core objective of the group is to develop an integrated ecosystem across sectors such as technology, infrastructure, transportation, real estate, and business consultancy. Each sister concern operates with its own expertise, but all share a common goal—ensuring quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

# Sub-Company | Business Verticals

## Zaraf Tech Hive Limited

As the technological backbone of the group, Zaraf Tech Hive Limited delivers a full range of online digital services. These include web solutions, social media management, digital support, and end-to-end event management. The company plays a key role in strengthening the digital presence and operational efficiency of businesses.

## Zaraf Transport

Zaraf Transport is a modern transportation service provider focused on introducing safe, reliable, and comfortable bus services across Bangladesh. Its primary commitment is to maintain high service standards while ensuring maximum convenience and comfort for passengers.

## Zaraf Real Estate

Zaraf Real Estate is engaged in the development of residential and commercial properties. Through modern design, sustainable construction practices, and planned development, the company is dedicated to creating improved living standards and a better business environment.

## Zaraf Consultancy Limited

Zaraf Consultancy Limited provides innovative business ideas, strategic guidance, and problem-solving services for businesses and entrepreneurs. Notably, the company offers consultancy services completely free of cost, playing an important role in supporting new entrepreneurs and business growth.
